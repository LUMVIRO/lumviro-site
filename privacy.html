Bannerivo Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 October 2026

Operator: Askar Fatakhov, an individual developer based in Ukraine, operating under the Lumviro brand. Lumviro is a brand, not a separate legal entity.

Contact: lumviro@gmail.com

About Bannerivo

Bannerivo lets you create and display text banners, save projects, export banner visuals, and record voice clips for playback. This policy explains how information is handled in the app.

Your banners and recordings

Your imported background images, banner text, phone numbers you voluntarily enter in an on-screen note, projects, settings, favorites, recent modes, and voice recordings are stored locally in the app's storage on your device. The app does not provide a developer-operated cloud account or cloud sync for this content.

Images you choose as backgrounds are copied into private app storage. We do not upload these images to our servers. Text and phone numbers displayed fullscreen are visible to anyone looking at your screen.

The microphone is used when you choose to record and grant microphone permission. Recordings are used for playback and voice effects. The app does not upload your recordings or banner text to a developer-operated server or intentionally send them to the advertising SDK. You can use the banner editor without microphone permission and can revoke microphone access through Android settings.

When you export and share a video or GIF, Android passes the selected file to the app you choose. That app handles your shared file under its own policies. Current banner exports do not include recorded audio.

Age group

On Android, the app asks you to select an age group. Your answer is self-declared and is stored only on your device; we do not request your date of birth or verify your identity. You can change the group in Settings. Changes pause advertisements and require closing and restarting the app to apply. Clearing the app’s storage removes this choice.

The app does not request advertisements for users who select under 13 or prefer not to say, or before an answer is provided. For users aged 13–17, the app requests restricted advertising treatment and marks them as under the age of consent for advertising. The SDK receives treatment flags; the app does not send the selected age band itself. Restricted advertising can still involve data processing.

Advertising and privacy choices

Bannerivo uses Google's Mobile Ads SDK to provide advertisements and Google's User Messaging Platform to manage applicable advertising privacy choices. Google's SDK may process network information, including your IP address and approximate location inferred from it, advertising and other device identifiers, interactions with the app or ads, and diagnostic information. Advertising partners use this information for ad delivery, measurement, and abuse prevention. Google states that its Mobile Ads SDK encrypts transmitted data using TLS.

Where applicable, you will be shown advertising privacy choices. You can revisit those choices through the ad privacy settings entry when it is available in Bannerivo. Android also provides controls for your advertising identifier. Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily stop all advertising-related data processing.

See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

The app stores the end time of an earned ad-free period locally so that it can continue after restarting the app.

Retention and deletion

Saved app content remains on the device until removed or replaced. Project recovery copies and autosaves can retain earlier content, including references to voice clips, after you edit or remove a recording from the current banner. Removing an item from the editor is not a secure erase of all recovery copies.

To remove all local app data, use Android's Clear storage option for Bannerivo or uninstall the app. This also removes your saved app projects and settings. Device backups or copies made outside the app, if any, must be managed separately. Files already shared to another app or saved elsewhere must be deleted separately. Advertising data handled by Google is subject to Google's retention practices and privacy controls; clearing Bannerivo's local storage does not delete data already processed by Google.

Contact and privacy requests

Contact lumviro@gmail.com with questions about this policy or privacy requests. Depending on your location, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, objection or restriction, and withdrawal of consent. Bannerivo does not maintain a developer cloud copy of your locally stored banners or recordings.

Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address, the name supplied by your email service, your message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to answer your request, investigate an issue, and handle privacy inquiries. Please do not send passwords, identity documents, or unrelated sensitive information. The app does not automatically attach your projects, recordings, or diagnostic logs to a support email.

Support correspondence is handled through Gmail. Google processes email-service data as described in its privacy policy. Access to the support mailbox is restricted to people authorized to handle support. We do not use support messages for advertising or marketing.

We retain correspondence for as long as needed to resolve the request and relevant follow-up issues, or to meet applicable legal requirements and establish, exercise, or defend legal claims. When those purposes no longer require it, we delete it from the support mailbox. You may request deletion by emailing us; we will explain any lawful reason that prevents immediate deletion. This describes deletion of information we control, not a guarantee of immediate erasure from every service-provider backup.

Legal bases and your rights

Where the GDPR or similar law applies, we rely on our legitimate interest in responding to support and privacy requests, subject to your rights and interests. Where processing is required by law, we rely on that legal obligation. We request advertising consent where legally required through the advertising privacy choices described above; these choices do not authorize unrelated uses of your content.

Depending on the applicable law, you may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal information we hold, object to processing based on legitimate interests, or withdraw consent without affecting the lawfulness of prior consent-based processing. You may also complain to your competent data protection authority. We may request information reasonably necessary to verify a request, but do not ask you to send identity documents with an initial inquiry. You can manage locally stored app content on your device; we do not hold a cloud copy that we can access or erase remotely.

International processing

Support messages are received by the developer in Ukraine. Google's advertising and email services may process information in other countries. Google's description of its international-transfer safeguards is available in its data transfer frameworks. That description concerns Google's services; it does not by itself establish compliance for every processing activity of the developer.

## Children and audience

Bannerivo is intended for teenagers aged 13 and above and adults. It is not designed specifically for children under 13. If you believe a child has supplied personal information directly to our support mailbox, contact lumviro@gmail.com so we can review and address it.

## This website

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting it sends ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, to GitHub to deliver and secure the page. See GitHub’s privacy statement. We do not add advertising, analytics scripts or tracking cookies to this policy page.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date will identify the latest published version.
