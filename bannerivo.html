A few things to know.

Where can I download Bannerivo?

Bannerivo is preparing for Google Play and is not publicly available yet. We’ll add the store link here after release. There is no iOS release announced for Bannerivo.

Which languages are supported?

The current build supports English, Ukrainian, Russian, German, Spanish, French, Italian and Polish.

Can I leave a note on my phone?

Yes. You can display a message and an optional phone number. The information is visible to anyone who can see the screen, and the feature does not lock or protect your phone. Avoid leaving your phone in direct sunlight or behind glass where it can overheat.

Are the signal effects for emergencies?

No. Light and sound effects are for decoration and entertainment, not official emergency signals. Some modes use bright flashes and loud sounds.
